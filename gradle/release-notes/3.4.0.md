InvSort polish and clearer sorting feedback.

- Adds support for final Minecraft `26.2` and `26.3-snapshot-1`.
- The InvSort rules screen now shows player inventory slots in the normal
  inventory shape: main rows first, then the hotbar at the bottom with a small
  gap above it.
- InvSort now tells you when sorting cannot finish because there is no free
  inventory slot for the carried stack.
- Click a physical tracked container location in an expanded InvSearch result
  to highlight that exact block in the world. On 26.x, the marker has a solid
  yellow outline and translucent fill visible through walls.
- Selecting a tracked location now marks every matching container in your
  current dimension by default; use its `Highlight` button to mark only one.
  Highlights automatically disappear after one minute.
